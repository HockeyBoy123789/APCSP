# AP CSP (csp-2025) — Lesson-by-Lesson Video Study Guide

This guide maps every one of the 97 instructional lessons in Code.org's AP Computer Science Principles course, csp-2025, to a YouTube video — one row per lesson, each with a verdict on how well the video serves that lesson. It follows the csp-2025 curriculum exactly: 10 units and 97 instructional lessons, excluding end-of-unit surveys and the two alternate-path lessons. Code.org's site notice says there are no changes to the curriculum for 2026–27 and that csp-2025 is the current version, so this guide applies to 2026–27 as well.

## How to use this guide

- **Read the Verdict column first.** It tells you whether a video teaches the concept or merely shows a finished solution.
- **"Answer-key" videos are for checking work, not for learning.** Where coverage is answer-key or solution-forward (listed in [Gaps and weak spots](#gaps-and-weak-spots)), attempt the lesson first, then watch to compare reasoning.
- **Lessons with no video should be done from Code.org's lesson plan.** The guide does not substitute a generic video for these; use the Code.org lesson plan and the teacher forum instead.
- Lengths are given as `m:ss` and dates as `YYYY-MM-DD` upload dates.
